📖 LifeXP Guide

Welcome to the official LifeXP guide! Here is everything you need to know about leveling up your life.

1. Missions & Quests

Missions are your daily and weekly tasks. Completing them grants XP (Experience Points) and Coins.

  • Daily Missions: Refresh every day. Perfect for habits.
  • One-time Missions: Specific goals or large projects.
  • Difficulty: Harder missions yield more XP and Coins.

2. XP, Levels, and Coins

As you complete missions, you gain XP. Collect enough XP to Level Up!

  • Leveling Up: Showcases your overall progress in LifeXP.
  • Coins: Earned along with XP, Coins can be used to unlock premium avatars or rewards (future features).

3. Daily Streaks

Consistency is key. Logging in and completing tasks builds your streak.

  • Login Rewards: Log in every day to claim bonus XP and Coins!
  • Streak Multipliers: Higher streaks grant larger daily bonuses.

4. Achievements

Special milestones in your journey will unlock Achievements, displayed on your Profile.

  • Earn achievements by completing your first mission, reaching level milestones, or maintaining a long streak.

5. Leaderboard

Compete with the entire LifeXP community.

  • Global: Ranks everyone by all-time XP and Level.
  • Weekly: Resets every week. Who can earn the most XP this week?

6. Nova AI Coach

Your personal companion on the Dashboard.

  • Nova provides contextual advice based on your current missions, streak, level, and the time of day.
